# Safety and boundaries

The skill is a engineering design-review and evidence scaffold. It is not a licensed engineer, safety signatory, code authority, or substitute for discipline-specific review.

Primary boundary:

Do not approve designs without discipline-specific standards, testing, uncertainty, safety, and qualified sign-off.

Human accountability:

Qualified engineering review owns technical adequacy, safety, ethics, compliance, and sign-off.

Stop when the source, rights, privacy, jurisdiction, accessibility,
or professional-review condition cannot be established. Use only original,
synthetic, public-domain, or permission-cleared materials. Never include
protected assessment items, answer keys, real patient/client records,
confidential institutional records, credentials, or secrets.
