## `OPT-68-ENGINEERING` — Reject an unsafe shortcut

**Learner target:** Frame requirements, compare design options, and reject a shortcut when safety or validation evidence is missing.

**Fictional design problem:** A team must choose between two public-facing device enclosures. One is cheaper and meets the visible size requirement but has not been tested under the stated temperature and load conditions.

**Learner task:** Build a requirements trace, identify the missing test, compare options, describe safety and stakeholder consequences, and prepare a design-review handoff that requests discipline-specific review.

**Required artifact:** design brief, requirements trace, options matrix, test / validation plan, risk register, safety / ethics review, handoff.

**Nonmodel:** Approving the cheaper design because it meets one visible requirement.  
**Feedback move:** Ask “Which requirement is untested, who could be harmed, and what evidence is required before approval?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent may generate options and inspect the trace; qualified engineering review owns safety and sign-off.

**Exit test:** Add a conflicting schedule constraint. Learner must document the tradeoff and preserve the safety requirement.
