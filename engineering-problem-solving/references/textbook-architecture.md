# `OPT-68-ENGINEERING` textbook-skill architecture

**Architecture ID:** `F2-ARCH-68-ENGINEERING-001`  
**Status:** `SPEC_DRAFT — DISCIPLINE SCOPE REQUIRED`  
**Promise:** Teach engineering problem solving and design as evidence-based, constraint-aware, ethical, communicative, and improvable work.

## Learner change

Before: the learner produces a technically plausible solution.  
After: the learner frames requirements, generates alternatives, tests and interprets evidence, accounts for safety and context, communicates tradeoffs, and documents improvement.

## Progression

`Frame → Specify → Generate → Analyze → Test → Decide → Communicate → Verify / improve`

## Unit map

| Unit | Work | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem, stakeholders, requirements, and constraints | design brief and requirements trace |
| 2 | Alternatives, tradeoffs, and risk | options matrix and risk register |
| 3 | Analysis, tools, and experimentation | analysis / test plan |
| 4 | Data, validation, and engineering judgment | results and uncertainty memo |
| 5 | Safety, ethics, context, and communication | safety / ethics review and stakeholder brief |
| 6 | Team handoff, verification, and improvement | design review and postmortem |

## Source boundary

ABET supplies accreditation-level outcomes and continuous-improvement requirements. NCEES supplies a discipline-specific licensure pathway. The first technical proof point must select a discipline before it claims technical adequacy.

## Human-plus-agent design

The agent may generate alternatives, inspect calculations, and compare requirements. The human owns safety, standards, tradeoffs, sign-off boundaries, and final design judgment. No agent output constitutes engineering approval.

## Evaluator seed

Dimensions: problem framing, requirements coverage, alternatives, technical evidence, testing, uncertainty, safety / ethics, communication, teamwork, and improvement.

## First drafting packet

Draft `ENG-001` will use a discipline-neutral design problem, then surface the point at which discipline-specific standards become necessary. The learner must document an unsafe shortcut and reject it with evidence.
